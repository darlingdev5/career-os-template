# CLAUDE.md — agents start here

You are working inside someone's Career OS. Read in this order before doing anything:

1. `01-context/profile.md` — who they are and their constraints
2. `01-context/metrics.md` — **the only numbers allowed.** Read before writing any bullet.
3. `01-context/voice.md` — how they write, and what they reject
4. `01-context/positioning.md` — the angles they compete on
5. `02-work/lessons.md` — mistakes already made. Do not repeat them.
6. `01-context/roles/` — the real stories, for the role in question

## Hard rules

- **Never invent a metric.** If a number isn't in `metrics.md`, it doesn't exist. Flag the
  gap instead of filling it.
- **Never claim experience they don't have.** Adjacent framing is fine; fabrication ends
  the process in round two.
- **One source of truth.** Update the canonical file, don't copy facts sideways.
- **Their voice beats your polish.** When a line feels flat, ask them to say it plainly
  and tighten that, rather than writing a prettier version of the flat line.
- **Date everything.** Convert "last week" to an absolute date before filing it.
- **Recap before the session ends.** Route what changed into the right file and append a
  dated entry to `CHANGELOG.md`.

containing the resume, any essays or free-text answers, outreach drafts, call notes,
and any artifacts built for it.
