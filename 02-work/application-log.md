# Application log

Every application, the angle used, and what happened. Append after every submission or
status change. This is how you stop re-learning the same thing about the same company.

| Company / Role | Angle | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Company — Role] | [Angle 1/2/3] | [Drafted / Submitted / Screen / Rejected / Offer] | [Comp band, location, where the artifacts live, what to remember] |

## Status conventions
- **Drafted** — materials exist, not sent
- **Submitted** — in their system, date it
- **In process** — note every touchpoint and who
- **Rejected** — write WHY if you know, or your honest guess. This is the most useful column.
- **Skipped** — and the reason. Skip discipline is a skill; log it so you can see it working.
