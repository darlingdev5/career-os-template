## Scoring rubric
Rate a role before investing in it:
- **Substance fit** — can you do the actual job, with proof?
- **Level** — is the title at or above your current scope? (Applying down wastes shots.)
- **Comp** — does the band clear your floor?
- **Location** — does it pass your constraints as written, not as hoped?
- **Mission** — would you be glad to explain this job at a dinner party?

Two roles fully prosecuted beats ten sprayed.
