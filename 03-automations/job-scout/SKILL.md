# job-scout

## Step 0 — Load
`02-work/target-companies.md` (targets, anti-targets, scoring rubric),
`01-context/positioning.md`, `01-context/profile.md` (constraints).

## Step 1 — Search
Search the target list first, then adjacent companies that fit the same angles. Prefer
official careers pages and the company's ATS over aggregators, which go stale and list
closed reqs as open.

## Step 2 — Hard gates (filter before scoring)
Drop immediately, and say why:
- Below the comp floor in `profile.md`
- Fails the location constraint as written
- On the anti-target list
- A level below current scope — applying down wastes shots
- A stated minimum in a domain they genuinely lack, with no referral path

## Step 3 — Score what survives
Substance fit · level · comp · location · mission. Rank them. Be willing to report that
nothing cleared the bar; a short honest list beats a padded one.

## Step 4 — Draft the top one or two
Hand off to `resume-tailor`. **Two roles fully prosecuted beats ten sprayed.**

## Step 5 — Log
Every role surfaced goes into `02-work/application-log.md`, including the skips and the
reason. Skip discipline is a skill; logging it is how you see it working.
